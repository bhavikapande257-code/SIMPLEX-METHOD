Looking Ahead

Future Prospects

SimplexSolve is an evolving project. Here is what we are planning to bring to the platform in the coming phases.

📊
Graphical LP Visualiser
For two-variable problems, render the feasible region, objective function contour lines, and the optimal corner point directly on an interactive canvas — watching geometry meet algebra.
🔢
Integer & Mixed-Integer LP
Extend the solver to handle integer programming via Branch-and-Bound, with complete tableau histories at every node — making MILP as transparent as the current simplex walkthroughs.
📄
Export to PDF / LaTeX
Generate a publication-ready PDF or LaTeX report of the full solution — every tableau, pivot annotation, and final answer formatted for academic submission or coursework documentation.
🌐
Multi-Language Interface
Localise the interface and mathematical explanations into multiple languages, lowering the barrier for students studying operations research outside English-language curricula.
📚
Problem Library
A curated bank of classic LP problems — diet, transportation, assignment, network flow — pre-loaded and ready to solve, with explanatory notes on their real-world context.
⚡
Sensitivity Analysis
After solving, report ranging for objective coefficients and right-hand-side values — showing how much each parameter can change before the optimal basis shifts.
Development Roadmap

What Comes Next

Our planned release milestones, working from the current foundation toward a fully-featured LP education platform.

Phase 1 — Current
Core Simplex Engine
Big-M and Two-Phase simplex with exact fraction arithmetic, full tableau walkthroughs, and support for ≤, ≥, = constraints and free variables. Deployed and functional.
Phase 2 — Near Term
Graphical Visualiser & Sensitivity Analysis
Add an interactive feasible-region plot for two-variable problems alongside post-optimality sensitivity ranging for objective coefficients and RHS values.
Phase 3 — Mid Term
PDF Export & Problem Library
Generate formatted PDF / LaTeX reports of complete solutions. Launch a curated problem bank covering classic OR applications from diet problems to network flow.
Phase 4 — Long Term
Integer Programming & Multi-Language Support
Extend to Branch-and-Bound MILP with node-by-node tableau histories. Localise the platform into additional languages to serve a global student audience.
